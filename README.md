# Privacy Policy for Furever

**Last Updated:** October 18, 2025  
**Effective Date:** October 18, 2025  
**Version:** 1.2

## Introduction

Welcome to Furever ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for our mobile application Furever (the "App").

**Important: Furever is a local, device-only application.** Your pet care data (pets, tasks, photos, schedules) is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

By using Furever, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Our Privacy-First Approach

**We Do NOT Collect or Store:**
- Your pet names, types, or photos
- Your care tasks, schedules, or reminders
- Your task completion records or care history
- Any personal information about you or your pets

**All of your pet care data stays on your device.** We have no servers, no databases, and no way to access your pet information.

## Information We Collect

Since Furever operates entirely on your device, we only collect minimal information necessary for subscription management:

### Subscription Information (via RevenueCat)

When you purchase a premium subscription, **only RevenueCat collects:**
- Anonymous user identifier (not linked to your personal identity)
- Subscription status (active, expired, trial)
- Purchase receipts and transaction information
- Device type and app version (for subscription validation)

**We do not collect this information directly.** RevenueCat handles all subscription management independently. See their privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Apple App Store Information

Purchase transactions are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple may collect:
- Payment information
- Purchase history
- Account information

This is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## What Stays on Your Device

The following information is stored **exclusively on your device** and never leaves it:

- Pet names, types, breeds, and photos
- All care tasks and schedules
- Task completion history
- Reminders and notifications
- Any notes or descriptions you add

**We cannot access this data. Period.**

## How Your Device Data is Protected

### Local Storage Security

- Data is stored using iOS Core Data framework with built-in encryption
- Data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
- Data remains sandboxed within the app and cannot be accessed by other apps

### iCloud Backup (Optional)

- If you enable iCloud backup on your device, iOS may automatically back up app data to your personal iCloud account
- This backup is controlled entirely by Apple and your device settings
- We have no access to your iCloud backups
- To manage this, go to: Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups

## Third-Party Services

### RevenueCat (Subscription Management Only)

We use RevenueCat solely to manage premium subscriptions. RevenueCat collects:
- Anonymous subscription status
- Purchase verification data
- Device and app version (for compatibility)

**RevenueCat does NOT have access to your pet care data.**

For more information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### Apple Services

The App uses standard iOS features:
- **Local Notifications:** Managed entirely by your device to send task reminders
- **App Store:** For subscription purchases
- **Photos (with permission):** To let you add pet photos from your device

These services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## How We Use Subscription Information

The minimal subscription data collected by RevenueCat is used only to:
- Verify your premium subscription status
- Unlock premium features in the app
- Process subscription renewals and manage billing through Apple

**We do not use this information for:**
- Marketing or advertising
- Analytics or tracking
- Selling or sharing with third parties
- Building user profiles

## Data Retention

### Your Pet Care Data
- Stored on your device indefinitely until you delete it
- Removed completely when you uninstall the app
- Not stored on any servers, so there's nothing for us to delete

### Subscription Data (RevenueCat)
- Retained by RevenueCat according to their policies
- Used only for subscription validation
- Not linked to your pet care data in any way

## Your Rights and Control

### Complete Control of Your Data

Since all pet care data is on your device, you have complete control:
- **View:** All data is accessible within the app at any time
- **Edit:** Modify any pet or task information directly in the app
- **Delete:** Remove pets, tasks, or uninstall the app to delete everything
- **Export:** Back up data through iCloud if you enable it in device settings

### Subscription Management

- **View Subscription:** Check status in Settings tab
- **Cancel Anytime:** Manage through App Store settings
- **Restore Purchases:** Use the restore button in Settings

### Notifications

- **Enable/Disable:** Control notifications in iOS Settings → Furever → Notifications
- **Per Task:** Choose which tasks have reminders when creating them

## What Happens When You Delete the App

When you uninstall Furever:
- **All pet care data is permanently deleted** from your device
- Subscription status remains with RevenueCat/Apple until cancellation
- iCloud backups (if enabled) follow Apple's backup retention policies
- No data remains on any servers (because we don't have any)

## Children's Privacy

Furever does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app operates entirely on the device:
- No data is transmitted to us
- No user accounts are created
- No age verification is required

Parents can safely allow children to use the app under supervision.

## International Users

Furever operates entirely on your device, regardless of your location. The only cross-border data transfer occurs through:
- **RevenueCat:** Subscription validation (see their privacy policy for data location)
- **Apple:** Purchase processing through the App Store

Your pet care data never leaves your device, so there is no international transfer of your personal information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted in the App with an updated "Last Updated" date. For significant changes:
- We'll display an in-app notification
- Continued use constitutes acceptance of the new policy

## Data Breach Notification

**Because your pet care data never leaves your device and we have no servers:**
- There is virtually no risk of a data breach from our side
- Your data security depends on your device security (passcode, biometrics)
- If RevenueCat experiences a breach affecting subscription data, they will notify affected users

To protect your device data:
- Use a strong device passcode
- Enable Face ID or Touch ID
- Keep iOS updated
- Don't jailbreak your device

## Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR/KVKK)

### Rights Related to Subscription Data:
- **Right to Access:** Contact RevenueCat for subscription information
- **Right to Erasure:** Cancel subscription and contact RevenueCat to delete subscription records
- **Right to Object:** Cancel your subscription at any time

### Rights Related to Pet Care Data:
Since your pet care data is stored only on your device:
- **Access:** View all data directly in the app
- **Rectification:** Edit any information in the app
- **Erasure:** Delete data within the app or uninstall
- **Portability:** Back up through iCloud (optional)
- **No Third-Party Processing:** Your data is never shared because it never leaves your device

You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.
